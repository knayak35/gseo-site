01 / About GSEO

Free, global and built around ambitious student research.

THE PUBLICATION
AMBITIOUS STUDENTS
READ AND HELP BUILD.

Our mission is to make high-quality student research and educational content freely accessible to learners worldwide.

Our vision is to become a leading global platform for student-led research, empowering young researchers to share knowledge, collaborate across borders and contribute meaningfully to education and innovation.

01

Excellence

We hold every publication to high standards of reasoning, evidence and communication. Students are encouraged to refine their questions, strengthen their methods and produce work they are genuinely proud to share.

02

Accessibility

Knowledge should be freely available to learners regardless of background or circumstance. We turn complex topics into clear resources and keep our work open to a global audience.

03

Integrity

Trust begins with honest research, transparent citations and responsible authorship. Contributors remain accountable for the accuracy, originality and ethical quality of everything they publish.

04

Collaboration

Better ideas emerge when people learn from one another. We connect students, educators and researchers across borders so that knowledge can be questioned, improved and shared.

05

Curiosity

Every meaningful project starts with a question. GSEO supports critical thinking, thoughtful exploration and the confidence to investigate ideas beyond the boundaries of a traditional classroom.

06 / Publish With GSEO

Turn curiosity into work the world can read.

YOUR NEXT QUESTION
COULD BECOME PUBLISHED WORK.

STEP 01

Prepare your contribution

Begin with a clear question or idea, write in professional English and provide reliable references wherever they are needed.

STEP 02

Submit original work

Send work that reflects your own thinking and avoids plagiarism, fabricated data, manipulated citations or false authorship.

STEP 03

Work with reviewers

The Academic Division evaluates accuracy, clarity, relevance, methodology and citation practices, then provides focused feedback.

STEP 04

Revise and publish

Respond to revision requests, strengthen the final piece and publish it for a worldwide audience with full authorship credit.

Eligible disciplines

Physics, chemistry, biology, mathematics, computer science and AI, engineering, finance, economics, international relations, psychology and mental health, environmental science, humanities and social sciences.

AI usage policy

AI may support brainstorming, editing and clarity, but authors must remain the primary creators, verify accuracy and accept full responsibility for published work.

Author ownership

Authors retain ownership of original work while granting GSEO permission to publish, distribute and promote it with full credit.
